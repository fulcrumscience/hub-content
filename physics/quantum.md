# Quantum Systems

ML for quantum computing and quantum physics — error correction, state tomography, variational algorithms, and quantum simulation.

## Overview

Machine learning intersects with quantum physics in several ways:

- **Quantum machine learning** — Quantum circuits as ML models
- **ML for quantum control** — Optimizing gate sequences, error mitigation
- **Quantum simulation** — ML surrogates for many-body quantum systems
- **Neural quantum states** — Representing quantum wavefunctions with neural networks
