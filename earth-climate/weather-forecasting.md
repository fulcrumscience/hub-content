# Weather Forecasting

Neural network weather models — data-driven forecasting and benchmarks.

## Overview

Neural weather models trained on reanalysis data (ERA5) have demonstrated competitive or superior performance to traditional numerical weather prediction (NWP) at medium-range timescales. Key systems include:

- **GraphCast** (DeepMind) — GNN-based global weather prediction
- **Pangu-Weather** (Huawei) — Transformer-based weather forecasting
- **FourCastNet** (NVIDIA) — Fourier neural operator for weather
