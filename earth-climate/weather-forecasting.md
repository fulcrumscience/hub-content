# Weather Forecasting

Neural network weather models — data-driven forecasting and benchmarks.

<div class="hub-page-nav">

Neural weather models trained on reanalysis data (ERA5) have demonstrated competitive or superior performance to traditional numerical weather prediction (NWP) at medium-range timescales.
